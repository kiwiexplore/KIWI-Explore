## AI Development Transition

The Foundation Phase was planned and developed with ChatGPT (GPT-5.5).

At the completion of the Foundation, the project transitions to Claude Code as the primary development assistant due to its strengths in long-term software engineering and large codebase management.

This transition is a continuation of the same vision, architecture and roadmap.

All project knowledge has been documented and transferred through the project documentation and GitHub repository.

ChatGPT remains part of the project's history and may continue to be used for architecture discussions, brainstorming and strategic planning.

## Next Phase

The project now enters the Development Phase, focused on expanding KIWI into a fully modular AI Operating System.
